Questions

Good to
know.

Everything you need before your first session. Still curious? Book a tour and we'll show you around.

How does the free trial work?

Every membership starts with 7 days free — full floor access, classes and a facility tour. No card required to book, and no obligation to continue.

Am I locked into a contract?

Never. All IRONHAUS memberships are rolling and month-to-month. Cancel any time with no exit fees.

I'm a total beginner — is this for me?

Absolutely. Every new member gets an onboarding assessment and a coach to teach the fundamentals. The room is intense; the people are welcoming.

Can I bring a guest or buy a day pass?

Yes. Performance and Elite members receive guest passes, and single day passes are available at reception for visitors.

What are your opening hours?

Monday–Friday 05:00–23:00, and weekends 07:00–21:00. Elite members enjoy priority access during peak hours.
